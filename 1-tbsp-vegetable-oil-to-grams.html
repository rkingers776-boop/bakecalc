Convert tablespoons of cooking oil to grams — for greasing pans, counting macros, and half-batch recipes.

Gram Equivalent:

13.6 grams (13.6 g)

*1 US tbsp vegetable oil ≈ 13.6g. 16 tbsp per cup, 218g per cup ÷ 16 = 13.625g = 13.6g rounded.

1 Tablespoon Oil = 13.6 Grams. Here's When That Number Matters.

Most people don't weigh tablespoons of oil. They pour a glug into the pan and call it good. But there are three situations where knowing that 1 tablespoon equals 13.6 grams actually makes a difference:

  1. You're counting macros. A tablespoon of oil is roughly 120 calories and 14 grams of fat. If you're tracking nutrition and eyeballing the oil you cook with, you're probably off by 30-40 calories per meal. Over a week, that adds up.
  2. You're halving a recipe that calls for ⅓ cup oil. Half of ⅓ cup is 2 tablespoons + 2 teaspoons. That's 27 grams, not the 36 grams you'd get if you guessed "about 3 tablespoons." Weigh it once and the recipe turns out right the first time.
  3. You're greasing a pan and counting the oil in the recipe cost. That tablespoon of oil you swirl around the cake pan and dump out? Still counts toward your ingredient cost. At about $0.03 per tablespoon, it's not breaking the bank — but if you're costing a recipe for a cottage food business, every gram counts.

A funny thing about the tablespoon: for water, it's almost exactly 15 grams (14.8 to be precise). For oil, it's 13.6. The difference is about 1.2 grams — negligible for cooking, meaningful for baking. If a muffin recipe calls for 3 tablespoons of oil and you measure by pouring into a tablespoon, you're probably getting somewhere between 12 and 15 grams per spoonful depending on how full you fill it and whether there's a meniscus. If the recipe developer weighed their oil and wrote the gram amount, and you're measuring by volume, you're already off. That's the whole argument for gram-based recipes in one tablespoon.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q1: How many grams in 1 tablespoon of vegetable oil?

1 US tablespoon of vegetable oil = 13.6 grams (218g per cup ÷ 16 tbsp per cup). Rounded to one decimal place for practical kitchen use.

Q2: Is a tablespoon of oil the same weight as a tablespoon of butter?

Close but not identical — butter yields 14.2g per tablespoon (227g/cup), oil yields 13.6g (218g/cup). The 0.6g difference per tablespoon is about 4%. For 1-2 tablespoons in a recipe, it won't matter. For 8 tablespoons (½ cup), you're looking at a 5-gram difference, which starts to be measurable.
