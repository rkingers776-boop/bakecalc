How to Convert 50 Grams of Powdered Sugar to Cups

Powdered sugar, also called confectioners sugar or icing sugar, is dramatically lighter than granulated sugar. One US cup of unsifted powdered sugar weighs approximately 120 grams. This means 50 grams of powdered sugar translates to roughly 0.42 cups, or about 2/5 of a standard measuring cup. This small amount is typical for dusting desserts or making small batches of glaze.

The Mathematical Conversion Formula

The conversion uses the standard powdered sugar density of 120g per cup:

  • Total Cup Volume = Given Weight in Grams ÷ Standard Powdered Sugar Weight Per Cup
  • Powdered: 50g ÷ 120g/cup = 0.42 cups (roughly 2/5 cup)
  • Granulated: 50g ÷ 200g/cup = 0.25 cups (1/4 cup)
  • Packed Brown: 50g ÷ 220g/cup = 0.23 cups

Frequently Asked Questions

Q1: How many cups is 50g of powdered sugar?

A1: 50 grams of powdered (confectioners) sugar equals roughly 0.42 cups, or about 2/5 cup. Powdered sugar is very light and airy at only 120g per cup.

Q2: Should I sift powdered sugar before measuring 50g?

A2: If measuring by weight (50g on a scale), sifting beforehand does not change the mass. If measuring by cup volume, always sift powdered sugar first to remove lumps, then spoon it lightly into the measuring cup without packing.

Q3: Can 50g of powdered sugar be substituted with granulated sugar?

A3: Not directly. Powdered sugar contains cornstarch and dissolves differently. For frostings and glazes, the fine texture of powdered sugar is essential. For batters where sugar dissolves during baking, you can blend granulated sugar into a fine powder as a substitute.
