How to Convert 100 Grams of Brown Sugar to Cups

Brown sugar is unique among baking sugars because of its molasses content and moisture level. When converting 100 grams of brown sugar to cups, the standard culinary assumption is that brown sugar is firmly packed into the measuring cup. Packed brown sugar weighs approximately 220 grams per cup, making it denser than granulated white sugar (200g/cup) but heavier than powdered sugar (120g/cup).

The Mathematical Conversion Formula

The conversion divides the given mass by the standard packed brown sugar density:

  • Total Cup Volume = Given Weight in Grams ÷ Standard Brown Sugar Weight Per Cup
  • Packed Brown: 100g ÷ 220g/cup = 0.45 cups (just under 1/2 cup)
  • Granulated: 100g ÷ 200g/cup = 0.50 cups (1/2 cup)
  • Powdered: 100g ÷ 120g/cup = 0.83 cups

Frequently Asked Questions

Q1: How many cups is 100g of packed brown sugar?

A1: 100 grams of packed brown sugar equals approximately 0.45 cups, which is just under 1/2 cup. Because brown sugar is denser than white sugar when packed, it takes up less volume per gram.

Q2: Should I pack brown sugar when measuring by cup?

A2: Yes. Most recipes assume brown sugar is firmly packed into the measuring cup. Press it down firmly until the sugar holds the shape of the cup when turned out. This is why 1 packed cup of brown sugar weighs about 220g.

Q3: Can I substitute white sugar if I don't have 100g of brown sugar?

A3: You can substitute 100g of white granulated sugar, but you will lose the molasses flavor and moisture that brown sugar contributes. For a closer substitute, mix 100g of white sugar with about 1 tablespoon of molasses.
