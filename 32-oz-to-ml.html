Converting 32 Fluid Ounces (One Quart) to Milliliters

Thirty-two fluid ounces marks a major benchmark in the US liquid measurement system — it is exactly one liquid quart. This volume is common in bulk broth and stock recipes, large-batch soups, and commercial beverage containers. Using the standard multiplier, 32 fluid ounces converts to exactly 946.35 milliliters, just shy of a full metric liter.

The Mathematical Conversion Formula

The conversion uses the standard US fluid ounce to milliliter constant:

  • Total Milliliters (mL) = Fluid Ounces × 29.57353
  • 32 fl. oz. × 29.57353 = 946.35 mL

Frequently Asked Questions

Q1: How many mL is 32 fluid ounces (1 quart)?

A1: 32 US fluid ounces equals exactly 946.35 milliliters. This is equivalent to 1 full US liquid quart or 4 standard measuring cups.

Q2: Is 32 oz the same as 1 liter?

A2: Not exactly. 32 US fluid ounces equals 946.35 mL, which is slightly less than 1 full liter (1000 mL). A liter is approximately 33.8 fluid ounces, so 32 oz falls about 54 mL short of 1 liter.

Q3: How many pints and cups is 32 fluid ounces?

A3: 32 fluid ounces forms exactly 1 US liquid quart, which is equivalent to 2 liquid pints or 4 standard US measuring cups.
