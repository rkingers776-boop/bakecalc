Converting 200 Grams of Brown Sugar to Cup Measurements

When a recipe calls for 200 grams of brown sugar, you're working with nearly a full cup. Since packed brown sugar weighs 220 grams per cup, 200 grams equals about 0.91 cups — just shy of a full measuring cup. This near-cup quantity is common in large-batch cookie recipes, cake batters, and sweet sauces.

The Mathematical Conversion Formula

The volumetric formula uses the standard weight of packed brown sugar:

  • Total Cup Volume = Given Weight in Grams ÷ Standard Brown Sugar Weight Per Cup
  • Packed Brown: 200g ÷ 220g/cup = 0.91 cups (just under 1 cup)
  • Granulated: 200g ÷ 200g/cup = 1.00 cup (exactly 1 cup)
  • Powdered: 200g ÷ 120g/cup = 1.67 cups (roughly 1 2/3 cups)

Frequently Asked Questions

Q1: How many cups is 200g of packed brown sugar?

A1: 200 grams of packed brown sugar equals approximately 0.91 cups, which is just under 1 full cup. A full packed cup of brown sugar weighs about 220g, so 200g falls slightly short.

Q2: Why does brown sugar weigh more per cup than white sugar?

A2: Brown sugar contains molasses, which adds moisture and weight. When firmly packed into a measuring cup as recipes require, the molasses fills air pockets between granules, resulting in 220g per cup versus 200g for granulated white sugar.

Q3: How do I measure 200g of brown sugar without a scale?

A3: Without a scale, pack brown sugar firmly into a measuring cup until it's just under 1 full cup (about 7/8 cup or 0.91 cups). For the most accurate results though, a digital kitchen scale is strongly recommended for brown sugar.
