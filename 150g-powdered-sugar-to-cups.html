How to Convert 150 Grams of Powdered Sugar to Cups

150 grams of powdered sugar is a very common quantity in baking recipes, especially for frostings, glazes, and dustings. Because powdered sugar is so light — only 120 grams per unsifted cup — 150 grams actually exceeds one cup, coming in at exactly 1.25 cups or 1 1/4 cups. This is why frosting recipes that appear to call for modest weights of powdered sugar can produce surprisingly large volumes.

The Mathematical Conversion Formula

The formula divides the mass by the powdered sugar density constant:

  • Total Cup Volume = Given Weight in Grams ÷ Standard Powdered Sugar Weight Per Cup
  • Powdered: 150g ÷ 120g/cup = 1.25 cups (1 1/4 cups)
  • Granulated: 150g ÷ 200g/cup = 0.75 cups (3/4 cup)
  • Packed Brown: 150g ÷ 220g/cup = 0.68 cups (roughly 2/3 cup)

Frequently Asked Questions

Q1: How many cups is 150g of powdered sugar?

A1: 150 grams of powdered (confectioners) sugar equals exactly 1.25 cups, or 1 1/4 cups. At 120g per cup, powdered sugar is much lighter than granulated sugar.

Q2: How much frosting can I make with 150g of powdered sugar?

A2: 150g (1 1/4 cups) of powdered sugar can make enough buttercream frosting to cover approximately 12 cupcakes or a small 6-inch cake. A standard buttercream recipe typically uses about 2 cups (240g) of powdered sugar for a full batch.

Q3: Is 150g of powdered sugar the same as 150g of icing sugar?

A3: Yes. Powdered sugar, confectioners sugar, and icing sugar are all the same product — finely ground sugar with a small amount of cornstarch added. They can be used interchangeably in any recipe calling for 150g.
