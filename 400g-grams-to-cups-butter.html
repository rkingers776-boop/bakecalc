Scaling 400 Grams of Butter for Bulk Recipe Batches

Working with 400 grams of butter means you are likely scaling a rich pastry dough, brioche, or large cake batch — approximately 1 cup plus 3/4 cup (1.76 cups). Since standard US butter sticks cap at 113.4g each, and a cup of solid butter maxes out at 227g, larger butter weights require multi-stick and multi-cup calculations for accuracy.

The Mathematical Conversion Formula

The calculation divides butter mass by the density constant based on physical state:

  • Total Cup Volume = Weight in Grams ÷ Butter Component Per Cup
  • Stick Butter: 400g ÷ 227g/cup = 1.76 cups
  • Melted Butter: 400g ÷ 240g/cup = 1.67 cups
  • Whipped Butter: 400g ÷ 150g/cup = 2.67 cups

Frequently Asked Questions

Q1: How many cups is 400g of standard solid butter?

A1: 400 grams of standard stick butter equals roughly 1.76 cups — just shy of 1 3/4 cups.

Q2: How many sticks of butter do I need for 400g?

A2: 400g of butter equals about 3.5 US sticks (3 full sticks + half a stick). In cups, measure 1 full cup plus a generous 3/4 cup.

Q3: Does melted butter measure differently than solid butter at 400g?

A3: Yes. Solid stick butter at 400g measures 1.76 cups, but melted butter at 400g occupies only about 1.67 cups (240g/cup). Stick to solid measurements unless your recipe specifically calls for melted butter.
