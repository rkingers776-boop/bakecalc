Converting 250 Grams of Brown Sugar to Cup Measurements

When a recipe calls for 250 grams of brown sugar, you're working with slightly more than a single cup. Since packed brown sugar weighs 220 grams per cup, 250 grams equals about 1.14 cups — roughly 1 1/8 cups. This quantity is common in large-batch cookie recipes, cake batters, and sweet sauces.

The Mathematical Conversion Formula

The volumetric formula uses the standard weight of packed brown sugar:

  • Total Cup Volume = Given Weight in Grams ÷ Standard Brown Sugar Weight Per Cup
  • Packed Brown: 250g ÷ 220g/cup = 1.14 cups (roughly 1 1/8 cups)
  • Granulated: 250g ÷ 200g/cup = 1.25 cups (exactly 1 1/4 cups)
  • Powdered: 250g ÷ 120g/cup = 2.08 cups (roughly 2 1/12 cups)

Frequently Asked Questions

Q1: How many cups is 250g of packed brown sugar?

A1: 250 grams of packed brown sugar equals approximately 1.14 cups, which is roughly 1 1/8 cups. A full packed cup of brown sugar weighs about 220g, so 250g exceeds a single cup by a small margin.

Q2: Why does brown sugar weigh more per cup than white sugar?

A2: Brown sugar contains molasses, which adds moisture and weight. When firmly packed into a measuring cup as recipes require, the molasses fills air pockets between granules, resulting in 220g per cup versus 200g for granulated white sugar.

Q3: How do I measure 250g of brown sugar without a scale?

A3: Without a scale, pack brown sugar firmly into 1 full measuring cup, then add roughly 2 more tablespoons (1/8 cup). For the most accurate results, a digital kitchen scale is strongly recommended for brown sugar.
