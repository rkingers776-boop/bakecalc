Converting 3 Fluid Ounces to Milliliters

Three fluid ounces is a frequent measurement in cocktail recipes, small-batch sauces, and liquid extracts. Using the standard US fluid ounce conversion factor of 29.57353, 3 fluid ounces translates precisely to 88.72 milliliters. This small volume is just over one-third of a standard US cup.

The Mathematical Conversion Formula

The metric equivalent derives from multiplying fluid ounces by the standard conversion constant:

  • Total Milliliters (mL) = Fluid Ounces × 29.57353
  • 3 fl. oz. × 29.57353 = 88.72 mL
