Converting 24 Fluid Ounces to Milliliters

Twenty-four fluid ounces is a standard large-format volume, commonly seen in oversized water bottles, large soup and stock recipes, and bulk batch cooking. At exactly 3 US cups, this volume converts to precisely 709.76 milliliters using the standard 29.57353 mL per fluid ounce multiplier.

The Mathematical Conversion Formula

The metric conversion uses the standard US fluid ounce constant:

  • Total Milliliters (mL) = Fluid Ounces × 29.57353
  • 24 fl. oz. × 29.57353 = 709.76 mL
