24 oz to mL Converter

Convert 24 US fluid ounces to millilitres — three cups, two large drinks, and a reminder that ounces measure two different things.

Milliliter Equivalent:

709.76 mL

*Based on standard US Customary fluid ounce calculations (1 fl oz ≈ 29.57353 mL).

24 oz in mL — the short answer

Twenty-four US fluid ounces is 709.76 mL — 24 × 29.5735 = 709.7640. Under the imperial fluid ounce the same figure is 681.91 mL. Unlike most of the ounce conversions, 24 has no traditional unit behind it, and that turns out to be the most interesting thing about it.

Three Cups

Twenty-four fluid ounces is 3 cups, or 1.5 pints. Because 24 divides by 8 without a remainder, the cup equivalent is a clean whole number, and recipes that want this quantity usually just say three cups. The two phrasings are identical down to the millilitre.

It is also one of the few fluid ounce figures that lands on an integer number of cups — 8, 16, 24 and 32 all do, being multiples of eight. The intervening values, 12 and 20, land on halves and halves of halves.

Where 24 oz Comes From

Twenty-four ounces has no ancestor in the US customary system. It is not a pint, not a quart, not a fraction of a gallon — it is a packaging decision. That is why it shows up almost entirely in drink sizes:

  • Fountain drinks — the large cup at a convenience store sits between 24 and 32 fl oz.
  • Beer cans — the 24 oz can, sometimes called a quarter, is common in American craft brewing.
  • Sports drinks — 24 fl oz is a standard bottle size.
  • Shakers and bottles — 24 oz is the classic protein shaker capacity, marked in fluid ounces.

Twenty-Four Ounces of Flour Is Not Three Cups

Whatever else is true, the three-cup equivalence applies only to volume. Twenty-four ounces of weight is 680.39 g, and 680 g of dry ingredients varies enormously in how much space it takes up:

  • Flour — at 120 g per cup, 680 g is 5 and two thirds cups.
  • Granulated sugar — at 200 g per cup, 680 g is 3.4 cups.
  • Honey — 680 g is roughly 2 cups, because honey is dense.

Three ingredients, all labelled 24 oz, occupying between 2 and 5.7 cups. So the first task with any 24 oz figure is deciding which ounce is meant. Drinks, containers and thin liquids are almost always volume; flour, nuts and chocolate are almost always weight. The one reliable tell remains the letters fl, and where they are absent, the shape of the ingredient.

Who Actually Needs This Conversion

Two groups, mostly. People working out how much liquid is in a bottle they are drinking from, and people scaling a bulk recipe down to a home kitchen — a 24 oz formula reduced by four, for instance, becomes 6 fl oz, or three quarters of a cup.

Both cases benefit from the same discipline: decide whether the figure describes volume or mass before touching a calculator, because the two answers are not close to each other.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q1: How many mL is 24 oz?

A1: 709.76 mL for 24 US fluid ounces. Under the imperial fluid ounce used in Britain and Australia, 24 oz is 681.91 mL, about 4% less.

Q2: How many cups is 24 oz?

A2: Three cups, for a liquid — a US cup is 8 fluid ounces. Twenty-four ounces of a dry ingredient by weight is 680 grams, which is about 5 and two thirds cups of flour but only around 3 and a third cups of granulated sugar.

Q3: Is 24 oz a standard bottle size?

A3: Not in cooking. Twenty-four fluid ounces matches no traditional US measure — a pint is 16, a quart is 32 — so it belongs to drink packaging rather than recipe writing, where it shows up as fountain drinks, beer cans and sports bottles.
