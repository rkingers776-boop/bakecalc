How Many Cups Is 100 Grams of Cocoa Powder?

100 grams of natural unsweetened cocoa powder equals exactly 1 US cup. Dutch-process cocoa is the same — 100g = 1 cup. This is one of the few ingredients where the gram-to-cup conversion is a clean 1:1 (for 100g), which makes scaling recipes easier. Black cocoa is the exception — because it's more finely milled and aerated, it weighs only 95g per cup, so 100g of black cocoa equals about 1.05 cups (1 cup + 1 scant tablespoon).

The Conversion Formula (Grams → Cups)

The math is straightforward — divide by the per-cup gram weight:

  • Cups = Grams ÷ Grams-per-Cup
  • Natural Unsweetened Cocoa: 100g ÷ 100 g/cup = 1.00 cup
  • Dutch-Process Cocoa: 100g ÷ 100 g/cup = 1.00 cup
  • Black Cocoa: 100g ÷ 95 g/cup = 1.05 cups (≈ 1 cup + 1 scant Tbsp)

Cocoa vs Flour: The Density Difference Matters

All-purpose flour weighs 120g per cup. Cocoa powder weighs 100g per cup — that's 20% lighter. If you're adapting a recipe and think "cocoa and flour weigh about the same," you'll be wrong by 20%. A recipe calling for 100g cocoa and 100g flour means 1 cup of cocoa but only 4/5 cup of flour. By volume they look wildly different, but by weight they're the same. This is the strongest argument in favor of weighing dry ingredients: volume measurements hide these density differences.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q1: How many cups is 100g of cocoa powder?

A1: 100 grams of natural unsweetened or Dutch-process cocoa powder equals exactly 1 US cup (100g/cup). Black cocoa is lighter — 100g fills about 1.05 cups (95g/cup).

Q2: Why is cocoa powder lighter than flour?

A2: Cocoa powder has been defatted — most of the cocoa butter is hydraulically pressed out during processing. What remains is very fine, low-density solids that weigh only 100g per cup vs flour's 120g. This 20% density difference means substituting cocoa for flour by volume will dramatically change your recipe's structure.

Q3: Should I sift cocoa powder before measuring?

A3: Yes — cocoa clumps aggressively in storage. Those clumps create uneven packing that can skew your measurement by 10–15%. Sift or whisk vigorously before spooning into your cup. This converter assumes sifted, spooned, and leveled cocoa for the most accurate results.
