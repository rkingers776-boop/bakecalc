Converting 1 Fluid Ounce to Milliliters

The fluid ounce is the foundational unit of liquid volume in the US customary system. One single fluid ounce converts to exactly 29.57 milliliters using the standard culinary conversion factor. This base unit is critical for scaling small-batch recipes, measuring extracts, and dosing liquid medications accurately.

The Mathematical Conversion Formula

The conversion uses the standard US fluid ounce multiplier:

  • Total Milliliters (mL) = Fluid Ounces × 29.57353
  • 1 fl. oz. × 29.57353 = 29.57 mL
