Converting 100 Grams of Powdered Sugar to Cups

Powdered sugar (confectioners or icing sugar) is a finely ground sugar blended with a small amount of cornstarch to prevent caking. Because of its powdery texture, it weighs far less per cup than granulated or brown sugar. At 120 grams per cup, 100 grams of powdered sugar equals about 0.83 cups — more than 3/4 cup but not quite a full cup. This quantity is common in buttercream frostings and glazes.

The Mathematical Conversion Formula

The conversion uses the standard powdered sugar density of 120g per cup:

  • Total Cup Volume = Given Weight in Grams ÷ Standard Powdered Sugar Weight Per Cup
  • Powdered: 100g ÷ 120g/cup = 0.83 cups
  • Granulated: 100g ÷ 200g/cup = 0.50 cups (1/2 cup)
  • Packed Brown: 100g ÷ 220g/cup = 0.45 cups (just under 1/2 cup)

Frequently Asked Questions

Q1: How many cups is 100g of powdered sugar?

A1: 100 grams of powdered (confectioners) sugar equals approximately 0.83 cups. Powdered sugar is quite light at 120g per cup, so 100g is a bit more than 3/4 cup.

Q2: How many tablespoons is 100g of powdered sugar?

A2: Since 1 tablespoon of powdered sugar weighs roughly 7.5 grams, 100 grams equals approximately 13.3 tablespoons. In practical terms, that is 13 tablespoons plus 1 teaspoon of powdered sugar.

Q3: Does sifting affect the cup measurement of 100g powdered sugar?

A3: Yes, significantly. Unsifted powdered sugar scooped directly can compress, resulting in 120g per cup. Heavily sifted powdered sugar can weigh as little as 100g per cup. For consistent results, weigh on a scale or follow the recipe's sifting instructions carefully.
