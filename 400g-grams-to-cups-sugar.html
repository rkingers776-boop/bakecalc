Understanding 400 Grams of Sugar in Recipe Scaling

At 400 grams, you are dealing with exactly 2 full level cups (2.00 cups precisely). Because sugar types vary dramatically in density — granulated is dense and crystalline, powdered is light and aerated, and brown sugar contains sticky molasses — knowing the exact conversion for your specific sweetener is essential for consistent results.

The Mathematical Conversion Formula

The calculation divides the sugar mass by the specific density constant for each variety:

  • Total Cup Volume = Given Weight in Grams ÷ Base Ingredient Unit per Cup
  • Granulated White: 400g ÷ 200g/cup = 2.00 cups (2 cups)
  • Packed Brown: 400g ÷ 220g/cup = 1.82 cups
  • Powdered: 400g ÷ 120g/cup = 3.33 cups
