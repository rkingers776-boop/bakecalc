Frequently Asked Questions

Q1: How much is 4 oz of liquid in cups?

A1: Since 1 full cup equals 8 fl oz, a 4 oz liquid measure represents exactly 1/2 of a standard level customary cup.

Q2: What is 4 oz to mL rounded for quick cooking?

A2: In commercial kitchen environments where absolute chemical decimals aren't vital, chefs universally approximate 4 oz as 118 mL or 120 mL.

Q3: Does 4 oz of oil convert to the same mL as water?
